The thought process behind both concepts was to connect Charon from Greek mythology with Charon from John Wick, without making the logos look like direct references to either.

Both interpretations share a central idea: Charon is the guardian of a passage, someone associated with discretion, authority, and the rules of crossing from one world into another.

## Concept A — Controlled Coin Crossing

The idea: Passage comes at a price.

In Greek mythology, Charon is the ferryman who transports souls across the river into the underworld. Traditionally, a coin called an obol is associated with payment for that journey.

The logo incorporates this symbolism through:

- The circular C: Represents both Charon's initial and the ancient coin used to pay for passage.
- The central gateway: Symbolizes the boundary between two worlds.
- The red steps: Represent the journey or crossing from one side to another.
- The open circular structure: Suggests a controlled point of entry rather than a completely closed boundary.

The intention was to create a modern symbol with an ancient story behind it.

Underlying philosophy: Every passage has a threshold, and crossing it carries a responsibility.

## Concept B — Continental Seal

The idea: Authority doesn't need to announce itself.

This concept draws more heavily from Charon's character in John Wick, where he serves as the Continental Hotel's concierge.

He's composed, discreet, exceptionally professional, and operates within an institution governed by strict rules.

The logo reflects these characteristics through:

- The symmetrical architecture: Represents order, discipline, and stability.
- The central passage: A gateway between two worlds, connecting the mythology and the Continental.
- The surrounding curved elements: Create the impression of a protective institutional seal or insignia.
- The red diamond: Acts as a focal point, symbolizing the significance of crossing the threshold.
- The classical styling: Creates a sense of heritage, quiet authority, and timelessness.

Rather than depicting Charon himself, the logo is intended to feel like an insignia belonging to an institution that Charon would represent.

Underlying philosophy: True authority is exercised through composure, discretion, and an unwavering respect for rules.

## Why these two?

We deliberately selected two different interpretations of the same character.

| Concept A                           | Continental Seal                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Rooted primarily in Greek mythology | Rooted primarily in John Wick              |
| Focuses on the act of passage       | Focuses on the guardian of passage         |
| Symbolizes payment and crossing     | Symbolizes trust, discretion and authority |
| More modern and geometric           | More ceremonial and institutional          |

The colours and typography tie them together: black represents authority and restraint, red highlights the threshold or moment of significance, and the classical lettering reinforces the timeless character of the name.

Ultimately, neither logo is meant to depict Charon literally. The intention is for someone unfamiliar with the references to appreciate the design, while someone who understands the name discovers a deeper meaning.

That's what we were trying to achieve with both concepts: a logo that has a story worth telling, rather than just an attractive symbol.
